# VowOS Objection Handling Guide

## Objection: "Switching systems is too disruptive."
**Response:** "We understand that data migration is the scariest part of switching. That's why VowOS handles the heavy lifting. We have a structured data migration checklist and our platform's Supabase architecture allows us to run automated data integrity checks before you ever go live."

## Objection: "We already use [Generic Retail POS] and it's fine."
**Response:** "'Fine' often means your staff is creating manual workarounds for bridal-specific workflows. Does your current system natively support special order tracking with custom hollow-to-hem measurements? VowOS's Universal Product Catalog is built specifically for this."

## Objection: "Is the financial reporting accurate? We've had issues with split payments."
**Response:** "Absolutely. VowOS was built from the ground up with an integer-cents billing architecture. We don't use floating-point decimals, which means rounding errors in taxes, split payments, and layaways are mathematically eliminated."

## Objection: "What is this AI stuff? I don't want AI talking to my brides."
**Response:** "The VowOS AI Action Center doesn't replace your staff; it empowers them. It acts as an internal assistant, surfacing tasks—like reminding a stylist to call a bride whose dress just arrived—so nothing falls through the cracks."
