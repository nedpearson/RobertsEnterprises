# VowOS Sales Demo Script

## 1. Introduction (5 mins)
"Welcome to VowOS, the Operating System for Bridal & Specialty Retail. Unlike generic POS systems, VowOS is built specifically for the unique workflows of bridal shops—from that first appointment to final alterations."

## 2. The Universal Product Catalog (10 mins)
"Let's look at inventory. Bridal has complex SKUs—styles, colors, sizes, and hollow-to-hem measurements. Our Universal Product Catalog handles all of this natively, meaning you don't have to hack a standard retail system to track a dress order."

## 3. Financial Accuracy (5 mins)
"When dealing with high-ticket items, payment plans, and split commissions, math matters. VowOS uses an integer-cents billing engine on the backend. This guarantees zero rounding errors across quotes, payments, and tax calculations."

## 4. The AI Action Center (10 mins)
"Here is where VowOS acts as your digital manager. The AI Action Center actively monitors your data and surfaces tasks. It might prompt a stylist to follow up on a favored dress, or alert a manager that a vendor order is delayed. It's proactive, not just reactive."

## 5. Security & Reliability (5 mins)
"VowOS runs on a modern Supabase architecture. What that means for you is enterprise-grade security, row-level data protection, and real-time syncing across all your devices."
