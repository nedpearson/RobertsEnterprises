# VowOS Manager Training Checklist

## 1. Daily Operations
- [ ] Opening and closing the register.
- [ ] Managing the appointment calendar and stylist assignments.
- [ ] Handling returns, exchanges, and special orders.

## 2. Inventory Management
- [ ] Navigating the Universal Product Catalog.
- [ ] Receiving new inventory and printing barcodes.
- [ ] Initiating stock transfers between locations.
- [ ] Managing vendor purchase orders.

## 3. Customer Journey
- [ ] Creating and managing customer profiles.
- [ ] Tracking alterations and fittings progress.
- [ ] Managing layaways and payment schedules (integer-cents billing).

## 4. AI Action Center
- [ ] Responding to AI-generated tasks (e.g., "Follow up with customer X").
- [ ] Reviewing automated email/SMS communications.
