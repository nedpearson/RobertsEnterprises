# Demo Data Domain Map
**Project**: Roberts Enterprises / VowOS — FamousAI Version  

---

## 1. Store Locations (Synthetic)
1. **Magnolia Bridal — Downtown** (`demo-loc-01`): Flagship store, 6 fitting rooms, high appointment volume.
2. **Magnolia Bridal — Northshore** (`demo-loc-02`): Suburban store, high alteration throughput.
3. **Magnolia Bridal — River District** (`demo-loc-03`): Boutique boutique store, high VIP sales.

## 2. Business Module Schemas (Synthetic)
- **Staff Profiles**: 10 distinct synthetic staff members across Owner, Manager, Consultant, Inventory, Purchasing, Seamstress, Payroll Admin, and Pending roles.
- **Brides / Customers**: 15 synthetic bride profiles with complete wedding timelines, style preferences, budgets, and communication histories.
- **Inventory & Gowns**: 25 sample gowns and accessories with size/color variants, sample locations, and stock levels.
- **Purchase Orders**: 8 vendor purchase orders across Draft, Submitted, Confirmed, Shipped, and Delivered states.
- **Store Transfers**: 5 inter-store transfer tickets with transit tracking and discrepancy handling.
- **Alterations**: 6 active alteration tickets with fitting dates, seamstress assignments, and work progress.
- **Payroll**: Complete bi-weekly payroll cycle with timecards, overtime calculations, bonuses, commissions, and exceptions.
