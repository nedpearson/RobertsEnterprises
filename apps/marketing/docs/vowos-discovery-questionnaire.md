# VowOS Discovery Questionnaire

## Business Overview
1. How many store locations do you currently operate?
2. What is your average annual transaction volume?
3. What legacy POS or CRM system are you currently using?

## Inventory & Catalog
4. How many active SKUs do you carry?
5. Do you manage both off-the-rack sales and special orders?
6. How do you currently track alterations and seamstress workflows?

## Financials
7. Do you offer layaways, split payments, or financing?
8. How do you currently calculate commissions for stylists?
9. Are you experiencing issues with tax calculations or rounding errors in your current system?

## Technology & Operations
10. What hardware are you currently using (i.e., iPads, PC terminals, specific barcode scanners)?
11. How do you currently manage customer follow-ups and appointment reminders?
12. What is the biggest pain point in your daily operations today?
